# Code of Conduct

This is the organization-wide default. It applies to every `ninoverse`
repository, and to issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews within them.

## The standard

Be straightforward and be civil. Those are not in tension.

**Expected:**

- Critique the code, the design or the argument — not the person
- Assume the other party is competent and acting in good faith
- Say what you actually think, including disagreement, and say why
- Accept that a decision may go against you once it has been argued

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or bad-faith argument after a decision
  is settled

Technical disagreement is not a conduct issue. Being unpleasant about it is.

## Scope

Applies in every `ninoverse` project space, and to anyone representing the
organization in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern to [@nicolapasqua99](https://github.com/nicolapasqua99) —
directly on GitHub, or through GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow if you would rather
not contact a maintainer.

Reports are handled privately. The reporter's identity is not shared with the
person reported without the reporter's agreement.

Responses, roughly in order of severity: a private correction; a public
correction; a temporary block from the repository; a permanent ban from the
organization. Which one applies is a judgement call, and the maintainers own it.
